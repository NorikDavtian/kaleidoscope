Disco listens to the microphone: warp swells with the level, the mirrors kick on the bass, colour runs with the treble — and the parameters sway too, twist with the bass, moiré with the treble. Motion Drive scales the spin and colour push; Parameter Drive scales how far the field bends. Both leave your sliders untouched. Noise Floor is the gate — sound quieter than it counts as silence. Audio is analysed and discarded — nothing is recorded.
